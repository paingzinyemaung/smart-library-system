Features

Everything a modern library needs

From shelf inventory to digital lending — one sleek platform for admins, students and members.

Book & Category Management

Full CRUD for titles, authors and ISBNs, plus bulk Excel uploads and automatic low-stock alerts.

Issue · Return · Fines

Eligibility checks, live stock deduction, return tracking and automatic overdue fine calculation.

Digital Resources & PDF Reader

Upload e-books securely and read them straight in the browser — download with permission checks.

Advanced Search & Filters

Instant search by title, author, code or ISBN with multi-criteria filtering and fast pagination.

Analytics & Reports

Dashboards with borrow trends, top titles and active members — exportable to Excel and PDF.

Roles & Security

Spring Security protection with admin and member roles, so everyone sees exactly what they need.
